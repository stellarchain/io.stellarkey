# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the StellarKey community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behaviour

Examples include demonstrating empathy, respecting differing viewpoints and experiences, giving and accepting constructive feedback, taking responsibility and apologising when mistakes affect others, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behaviour includes sexualised language or attention, trolling or insulting comments, public or private harassment, publishing private information without permission, and conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and participation that conflict with this code and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Report conduct concerns privately through <https://stellarkey.io/security>. Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible, with attention to the safety and privacy of everyone involved.

## Scope and attribution

This code applies in project spaces and when someone officially represents the project in public spaces.
